A closer look

A place for the plan and what grew around it.

Every screen has a simple job: help you move through the day, keep what mattered, and find it again when you need it.

Planner

Plan in the shape that fits your family.

Choose a gentle rhythm or a time-and-weekday schedule. Reorder, skip, or carry things forward when a library trip or project takes the afternoon.

Capture & Portfolio

Keep the learning you want to remember.

Save a photo or a few words when a conversation, project, or unexpected rabbit hole matters. Over time, you have a record that tells the real story.

Attendance & Records

Keep the records without the end-of-year scramble.

See attendance by learner, catch up a past day, and keep the information you need close instead of rebuilding it later.
