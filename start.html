The whole app, at a glance

Here’s what you’re actually bringing home.

The planning, records, and moments you want to keep—together in one place.

01 · TODAY

Start with the day in front of you.

  • A home screen for attendance, what’s next, and your weekly Wrap-Up.
  • One calm place for every learner in your family.
  • Mark each child’s learning day with a tap.
  • See the next thing without holding the whole week in your head.
02 · PLANNER

Plan in the shape that fits your family.

  • Choose a flexible rhythm or a time-and-weekday schedule.
  • Add one-time, daily, weekly, or monthly activities.
  • Give activities their learners, subjects, and times.
  • Reorder, skip, or carry unfinished things forward.
03 · QUICK ADD

Catch the real day while it’s happening.

  • Moment: save a photo, note, or work sample.
  • Activity: put something on today’s plan.
  • Quick Log: count something that is already done.
  • Idea: keep a spark for later, with no date or pressure.
04 · PORTFOLIO

Build a record that feels like your family.

  • Take a photo or choose one from your library.
  • Add a few words, the date, and the subjects it touched.
  • Save one moment for one child—or several.
  • Turn a finished planner activity into a portfolio moment.
05 · RECORDS

Keep the records without the end-of-year scramble.

  • See attendance on a monthly, learner-by-learner calendar.
  • Catch up past days, then adjust any one later.
  • Set a family year and attendance goal that makes sense for you.
  • See subject coverage grow from completed planner activities.
06 · REVIEW & SHARE

Look back, share, and keep going.

  • Make a Friday Wrap-Up and weekly reflection from what you captured.
  • Create share cards for the moments you want to send.
  • Share a portfolio link instead of printing a pile of work.
  • Export a clean attendance PDF for the date range you need.
  • Open Claire’s Guide when the next homeschool question comes up.
