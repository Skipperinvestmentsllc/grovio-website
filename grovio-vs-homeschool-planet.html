Honest homeschool app comparison

grovio vs. Homeschool Planet

Both can bring more order to homeschool days. The difference is detailed school management or a small system that keeps records from taking over the day.

The short version

Homeschool Planet is the better direction for families who want lesson-level planning and a comprehensive school-management workflow. grovio is the quieter choice for families in low-to-moderate regulation states who need attendance, a flexible rhythm, a portfolio of real learning, and a clean record when someone asks.

What mattersgrovioHomeschool Planet
PlanningWeekly rhythm plus one-off itemsDetailed, lesson-level planning
RecordsAttendance, portfolio moments, and PDF exportA broader academic-management workflow
Daily effortAbout five minutes: mark, glance, captureMore detail can mean more setup and maintenance

When to pick Homeschool Planet

Pick Homeschool Planet if you need detailed lesson scheduling, curriculum progress, grades, or a fuller academic-management system. Those are valuable jobs; they are simply not the jobs grovio is trying to do.

When grovio fits better

Pick grovio if the thought of setting up another whole system makes your chest tighten. It gives you enough structure to mark attendance, capture the evidence in your camera roll, and keep the week from disappearing — with no streaks and no guilt when a day goes sideways.
