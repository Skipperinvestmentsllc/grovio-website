How do I start homeschooling my child?

Claire

A preview from the grovio Guide

Okay, first: if you're asking this question, you're already doing better than you think.

I remember standing in my kitchen after we made the decision, thinking, "Now what?" I thought I needed a plan. A curriculum. A schedule that looked official.

I had none of it.

And honestly? That ended up being okay.

Here's the thing: starting homeschooling isn't about getting everything right from day one. It's about taking the first step without needing the whole picture figured out.

So here's where I'd actually start if I were sitting at your kitchen table right now. Before any curriculum, spend the first week just watching. What does your child light up about? When do they focus best, first thing in the morning or after lunch? What makes them shut down?

That one week of noticing will teach you more than any boxed curriculum could. Homeschooling works precisely because it bends to your specific child, and you can't bend it until you know what you're actually working with.
