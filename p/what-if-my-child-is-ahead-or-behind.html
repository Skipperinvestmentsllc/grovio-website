What if my child is ahead or behind in certain subjects?

Claire

A preview from the grovio Guide

My older child was reading chapter books at five but still counting on fingers at seven.

My younger one was the opposite.

For a while, I worried about both of them.

Then I realized: this is completely normal. And honestly, it's one of the best reasons to homeschool.

Because when you're not tied to a grade level, you get to meet each child exactly where they actually are, instead of where a textbook insists a seven-year-old should be.

My chapter-book reader who still counted on her fingers? We simply let her race ahead in reading while we took math slow, with no shame attached to either one. By nine, the finger-counting was just gone. Nobody had to panic. She needed time, and homeschooling is the one setting that can actually hand a child time.
