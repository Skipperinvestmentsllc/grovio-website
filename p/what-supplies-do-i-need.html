🌱 Getting Started

What supplies do I actually need to begin?

Claire

A preview from the grovio Guide

I'm going to save you from something I did our first year: buying everything.

I walked into a teacher supply store and left with bins, organizers, charts: all of it.

Most of it never got used.

Here's what you actually need at the beginning: something to write with, something to write on, and a library card.

That's genuinely it to begin. Something to write with, something to write on, and access to books. Everything else you add slowly, and only when an actual need shows up, never because a list told you to.

The reason I push this so hard is that the bins and charts and laminated everything aren't just a waste of money. They create pressure. A shelf full of unused supplies quietly tells you you're behind, that you bought all of this and still aren't doing it right.
