A little about grovio

For the things that matter, even when the day was ordinary.

grovio is a private place for homeschool families to hold plans, attendance, moments, and records as life actually happens. It helps parents see what is already growing — without turning home into a performance.

Who we’re looking for

Warm voices with real communities.

We welcome homeschool, parenting, family-life, learning, co-op, and education creators. Engaged, active communities matter much more than huge follower counts.

Creators often have a regular reach of around 100 people through Stories or 1,000 recent short-form viewers. We consider audience fit, engagement, content quality, and recent activity together.

What we ask of partners

  • One original Reel or TikTok sharing your real experience with grovio.
  • Two Story sequences, each with at least three frames and your unique link or code.
  • Honest personal experience, creative freedom, and a clear disclosure such as “Ad,” “Sponsored,” or “grovio gave me Premium access.”

FAQ

A few good questions.

Is this a paid partnership?+

No. This is a gifted creator partnership with grovio Premium access, not employment or cash compensation.

Do I need a large following?+

No. A thoughtful, active community matters more than a large audience.

What do I receive if approved?+

Approved creators receive 3 months of grovio Premium and a unique grovio link and code for their community.

How do I unlock the additional nine months?+

When 10 separate referred families become confirmed paid grovio Premium members, you unlock 9 additional months.

What counts as a confirmed paid referral?+

A separate, valid user who redeemed your unique follower offer and completed a real paid purchase after any trial period. Each referred user can count once.

When will I hear back?+

Every application is reviewed personally. We will be in touch within 5–7 business days.
