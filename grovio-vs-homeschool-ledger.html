grovio vs. Homeschool Ledger

Both are mobile-first ways to keep up with homeschool records. The choice is deeper compliance coverage or a lighter practice that helps you see learning as it happens.

Homeschool Ledger speaks directly to confidence and compliance across all 50 states. grovio is for families in low- to moderate-regulation states who want attendance, a growing portfolio, a weekly rhythm, and evidence ready to export without building a large system.
