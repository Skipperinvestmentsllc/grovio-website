About grovio

Homeschooling is one of the most personal things a family can do. The days are full, the moments matter, and the last thing anyone needs is an app that turns it all into busywork.

grovio exists to stay out of the way. It's a calm, simple place to track learning, capture the moments worth keeping, and stay on top of whatever your state asks of you — without the spreadsheets, the scattered notebooks, or the camera roll full of work you'll never find again.

What grovio believes

Homeschooling doesn't need to be optimized. It needs to be supported. Most tools in this space were built for classrooms and then pointed at families. grovio was built the other way around — for the way homeschool days actually unfold. Slow mornings. Spontaneous tangents. Deep dives. Off days. A rhythm that bends with your life instead of fighting it.

Your records should work for you, not the other way around. Attendance, portfolio moments, the proof that this year happened — grovio keeps it organized quietly in the background, so it's there when you need it and invisible when you don't.

Your data is yours. grovio is built to keep your family's information private and in your hands. No selling, no ads, no mining what you record for anything other than helping you.

Calm over clever. No streaks to maintain, no guilt-trip notifications, no gamified pressure. grovio is designed to lower the noise, not add to it.

Who it's for

grovio is for the parent doing the real work of homeschooling — the one who wants to remember this season, stay compliant without stress, and look back at the end of the year with proof that they did more than they gave themselves credit for. Whether you're brand new and second-guessing everything or years in and just tired of piecing it together, grovio is built to meet you there.

Who makes grovio

grovio is built by a homeschool parent who got tired of piecing it all together — three notebooks for attendance, a camera roll full of unsorted work, and no app that felt made for families instead of schools. So they made the thing they wished existed.

The grovio Guide

Alongside the app, grovio publishes a free library of homeschool guidance written by Claire — covering everything from getting started to staying sane in year three. Every article is free, no account required.
