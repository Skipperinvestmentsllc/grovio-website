How does the trial work?

After setup, you get 14 days of Premium automatically, with no credit card required to begin. Use that time to build your planner, try unlimited records, and see whether cloud backup, exports, and Wrap-Ups are worth keeping.

What if Premium lapses?

Anything you created during your Premium trial stays yours. If you do not subscribe, grovio returns to the free plan: your existing planner can still be used, and Premium-only features like unlimited new storage, cloud backup and sync, exports, and Wrap-Ups pause until you upgrade.

Can I restore a purchase?

Yes. Use Restore purchases in Settings to reconnect the subscription attached to your Apple ID or Google account.

Does one subscription work everywhere?

Billing is handled by Apple and Google. A subscription belongs to the store account that purchased it.
