Honest homeschool app comparison

grovio vs. Homeschool Tracker

Both keep records. The real question is whether your family needs deep academic and compliance tools, or a calmer way to see what already happened.

The short version

Homeschool Tracker is the more thorough system for grades, assignments, transcripts, report cards, and detailed logs. grovio is the simpler choice for families in low-to-moderate regulation states who want attendance, a photo-and-notes portfolio, a gentle planner, and a clean PDF when an evaluator asks.

What mattersgrovioHomeschool Tracker
Daily recordsOne-tap attendance and real learning momentsDetailed assignments, grades, and hours
PlanningFlexible rhythm plus individual itemsLesson-level scheduling with grades
ReportingPDF attendance exportTranscripts, report cards, and detailed reports
TimeAbout five minutes a dayMore setup for more detail

When to pick Homeschool Tracker

Pick Homeschool Tracker if you need official academic records, GPA calculation, curriculum-aligned progress, detailed assignment scores, or high-regulation compliance. It is built for that depth; grovio is not.

When grovio fits better

Pick grovio if your records are scattered across notebooks and your camera roll, and you want a system you will still use in October. Mark attendance, save a moment, and end Friday with a summary of what counted.
