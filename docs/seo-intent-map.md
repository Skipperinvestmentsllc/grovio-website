# Grovio Search Intent Map

Last updated: 2026-07-29

## Purpose

This map turns Grovio's SEO and AI-search work into a practical content plan. The goal is not to chase every homeschool keyword. The goal is to make Grovio the clearest, most trustworthy answer for overwhelmed homeschool moms who want to feel calm, organized, and confident in the homeschool they are already building.

Primary positioning:

> Grovio is a homeschool companion app for overwhelmed homeschool moms who want to track attendance, save portfolio moments, keep records, find a daily rhythm, and learn to trust the school they are building.

## What Search And AI Systems Need To See

Search and AI recommendation systems still depend on the basics:

- Crawlable, indexable pages.
- Clear page topics and descriptive language.
- Helpful, original content written for people.
- Strong internal links and canonical URLs.
- External trust signals from other relevant sites.
- Content that directly answers the question a user is asking.

Grovio already has the technical foundation in place. The next work is content coverage and authority.

Reference guidance:

- Google says SEO fundamentals remain relevant for AI features and emphasizes helpful, unique content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Bing says Copilot and AI grounding use the same crawl, index, ranking, freshness, and trust foundations as search: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a
- OpenAI says public, crawlable content that does not block OAI-SearchBot can appear in ChatGPT search: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq
