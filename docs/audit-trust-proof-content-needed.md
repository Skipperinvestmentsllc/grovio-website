# Trust proof content needed

The homepage now uses the verified App Store rating of **5.0 out of 5 from 5 ratings**, checked August 30, 2026, plus three short public App Store review excerpts. The cards are explicitly labeled as App Store reviews.

## Testimonial wall (D3.2)

Collect permission before creating a more personal testimonial section. For each of three to five families, collect:

- First name
- State, if they are comfortable sharing it
- A photo they approve for the website
- One concrete sentence in their own words
- Written permission and the date it was granted

Do not add photos, locations, or unverified claims from an App Store listing.

## Rating or quantity proof

Before updating an App Store or Google Play badge, record the source URL, rating, review count, and date checked. Google Play currently exposes only “5+ downloads” publicly; it does not show a public rating or review count to use. Wait for a meaningful public rating or a verified aggregate from Play Console before adding a Google Play rating claim.

## User-count badge and avatars (D3.1)

Do not publish a customer count until it comes from a verified analytics or store source and has a date recorded. Collect written permission before using any customer avatar. The audit calls for eight to twelve real avatars next to a current “Joined by [X] homeschool families” count; neither the count nor image permissions are available in this repository.

## Product visuals (D1.5 and Year Target)

Supply three current screenshots from the app before publishing these visuals:

- A shareable portfolio card as it appears when shared to Instagram.
- An exported PDF attendance record with the administrator certification block visible.
- The Year Target progress view showing a learner’s days and/or hours on-pace status.

The features page has the Year Target copy and a source-stub comment, but intentionally does not present a made-up app screen.

## Third-party validation (D3.5)

Do not add Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, press, award, or partnership badges until the underlying public listing or coverage exists. Link only to the original source and record the date it was checked.

## Real-device verification (D2.5)

The audit caps mobile experience at partial until the site is tested on a real iPhone and Android device. Check the homepage navigation, store badges, feature carousel, review cards, pricing cards, and comparison tables at their normal browser sizes; record any layout issues before the next audit.
